Subscription service, free trial & booking fee 
Subscription service (boarder) 
After the free trial expires, the subscription automatically starts. This means the payment is taken, 
either monthly or annually (prefer annual only, unless reason to oﬀer both options). 
Boarders should be able to cancel their subscription at any time, however, they will not receive a 
refund, instead they will maintain full access until the subscription renewal date lapses.
Upon the subscription lapsing, the boarder will still be able to log into their account but have 
limited access to services:
-No live advert visible to the public
-Able to view all pet owner conversations, booking overviews and payments. 
-Not able to take new payments
-Limited messaging with existing customers only (need to be able to discuss current/future 
bookings still)
Free trial (boarder) 
After registering and creating their advert, they are blocked by a paywall before they can publish. 
The paywall should state the monthly or annual cost but note a 30 day free trial period and that 
no payment will be taken at this time, however payment details must be collected to continue.
Boarders can cancel the free trial at any time, however, they will maintain full access until the free 
trial date lapses.
Upon the free trial lapsing, the boarder will still be able to log into their account but have limited 
access to services:
-No live advert visible to the public
-Able to view all pet owner conversations, booking overviews and payments. 
-Not able to take new payments
-Limited messaging with existing customers only (need to be able to discuss current/future 
bookings still)
Booking fee (pet owner) 
Sharetribe provided 500 free transactions per month, with all subsequent transactions being 
charged to Pets Places at c. 14p per transaction. 
Pet owners pay a booking fee for each booking/payment. 
We looked a ﬂat fee of 50p per transaction, however, if multiple payments are made per booking 
(deposit, remaining balance, adjustment to booking dates), this could add up and frustrate pet 
owners and risk business for boarders.
We considered a percentage rate per transaction, however, large bookings would accrue a large 
booking fee unfairly. 
This is a large income revenue stream so needs to be done smartly.

Messaging system & booking overview 
Once a pet owner has sent their initial enquiry, it will appear in the pet owner and boarders 
internal inbox. They should be able to see when their message has been read. Each conversation 
with a diﬀerent boarder or pet owner within the inbox should show a summary of the pet owners/
boarders (and company) name, proﬁle picture, a preview of the latest message. 
Clicking on the proﬁle picture of the pet owner or boarder, takes you to their proﬁle. Clicking on 
the message preview takes you to the conversation. The conversation shows all messages. To the 
side is a summary of the enquiry details (dates in written form, pet proﬁle pictures). The boarder 
also has the option to send a booking overview and payment request at any time. 
To send a booking overview and payment request, boarder clicks ‘send new booking overview 
and payment request’ button. A new window pops up, displaying a calendar to select the dates.
Below, the pets proﬁle pictures, names and pet type are displayed, with the text ‘Select pets for 
boarding’. Click on each pet to choose them. A ring of colour displays around image to show it 
has been selected. 
Below is a free text box to add additional information. 
Below is a box to display a total cost, and another for the deposit amount. The boarder can 
choose to add a percentage or monetary amount (percentage amount will be calculated from total 
cost entered).  If left blank, no deposit option displays to pet owner. Below, is a submit button.
When a payment request is sent to the pet owner, the initial enquiry information is replaced on the 
side by the information used in the booking overview. This will display the selected dates in 
written form, the pets proﬁle pictures and names, selected by the boarder, and any free text 
entered by the boarder. Clicking on the pets proﬁle picture will show the information related to 
that pet. Underneath, they will see the cost, and the deposit amount, with the option to select 
which one they wish to pay. If no deposit is added by the boarder, the full amount only will be 
displayed. If a deposit is paid, a breakdown of the remaining balance will display.
Once paid, the pet owner and boarder will be notiﬁed of successful payment and conversation 
can continue as needed. The booking overview and payment details will remain on the side.
The boarder can also send a ‘how to prepare for your pets stay’ template. This can include  
business opening hours (by appointment only), parking instructions, nearby train stations and a 
visual list of items to bring. 
If a pet owner wants to make multiple bookings at one time, or make additional bookings at a 
later date, they are likely to message within the same conversation, rather than send a new 
enquiry through the boarders advert. Upon agreeing the new booking(s) boarders can create 
additional booking overviews and payment requests, by clicking the ‘send new booking overview 
and payment request’ button.
Boarders should be able to update the booking overview at any time, but the pet owner should 
always be notiﬁed of this. Any updates to booking dates should not altar the amount already paid 
for that booking (if deposit done by percentage).
If more than one booking has been made within the conversation, the system should be able to 
cycle through overviews made, automatically displaying the booking with the nearest date. 
Once a booking has passed, it should come oﬀ of the messaging system but be stored in a 
booking history section of the log in, for both pet owner. The boarder should also be able to 
access booking history easily, but may need to have several ﬁltering sections to narrow down 
customers.

Reviews & private feedback 
After a booking is complete, an email or notiﬁcation is sent to the pet owner to review their latest 
stay.
There should be simple 1-5 star rating options for diﬀerent parts of the experience:
-Cleanliness
-Accuracy
-Communication
-Overall
Pros to multiple star options:
•Pet owners can look at the rating of what is most important to them.
•Boarders can see where they need improvement easily.
•Pets Places can see where boarders need help easily, if we need to step in, following lots of bad 
reviews.
Cons to multiple star options:
•More steps to completing a review
•Repetitive if someone is using the boarder regular and completing the review each time.
If a pet owner is repeatedly using the same boarder, and has previously reviewed them, we may 
want an option to opt out of future review email requests. There should still be the option to easily 
review that boarder on the website, as experiences can change.
Reviews should only be possible from veriﬁed customers. 
Host should be able to respond to any free text reviews. 
Private feedback should be possible for owners to leave boarders feedback, separate from 
reviews, to remain private from anyone else seeing. The boarder should be able to respond with 
one message. Any further messages would then need to be taken to the messaging system.
